How the ratings work

Each player has a rating that predicts their chance of winning a single frame against a given opponent: a 100-point gap means the stronger player wins about 64% of frames. After each match both ratings move by how far the share of frames won differed from that prediction, scaled up a little for longer matches, because a 10–3 tells us more than a 4–3.

A frame-win chance converts into a match-win chance for any length, so the same two players get different odds over best-of-7 and best-of-35. Over longer matches the better player is more likely to win.

Shoot Out matches (single frames scored in points) and seniors events are left out. On matches the model hadn't seen yet, it picks the winner about two times in three.
